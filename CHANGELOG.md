# Changelog

## 0.1.1-beta
- **A new accent colour: hot magenta.** The tab underline, progress bars, selected filters, the launcher button's frame on mouse over
  and the notification stripe follow it. The main buttons (Post Bounty, Take Job and the like) are now filled with the colour, with
  light text, a lighter border, a lighter shade on hover and a darker one when pressed. Cancelled and expired bounties stay grey,
  finished ones green.

## 0.1.0-beta
- First playable version for TBC Classic: board, Post Bounty, Take Job, cancel, expiry, guild sync, live bag
  progress, trade verification (delivered / paid), My Bounties, My Jobs, History, toasts.
- Help bounties: no item, a few words about what is needed; "Offer Help", "I helped" (helper) and "Confirm help" (creator);
  gold is paid by trade as before.
- Kill on Sight bounties: a player name and a number of kills; the hunter's client counts PARTY_KILLs of that player
  (own or group), the creator confirms and pays by trade.
- Guild goals: an officer posts a shared goal ("400x Thorium Bar"), anybody contributes by trading to the recipient
  (the creator or a named guildmate), one progress bar for everybody, the recipient pays the reward for every item.
  Who can post is a setting (rank).
- Craft bounties: an item to craft, a reward, and who brings the materials (the crafter by default). The finished
  items are traded for the gold and verified like a farm bounty.
- A colour for each kind of bounty (tag and a stripe on the card; can be switched off), a choice of notification
  sounds with a preview, and "Clear history" / "Clear finished" (cleared bounties are not fetched again from the guild).
